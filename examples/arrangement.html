Pizza arrangement: symmetry and contact

Each ring marks where a topping would have to be for the pizza to be perfectly symmetric: reflected across the best mirror axis, or turned by the best rotation. The ring's colour says whether a piece of the same topping is actually there. The score is the average distance from each ring to the nearest matching piece, divided by the same distance for random pizzas with the same toppings: 0 is perfect symmetry, 1 is no more symmetric than chance. The contact panel joins pieces whose outlines touch or overlap; faded pieces touch nothing, and a number gives the height of a pile of three or more. A negative gap means pieces overlap. Groups come from the group column.
