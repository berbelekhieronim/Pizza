Pizza arrangement: symmetry and contact

Each ring marks where a topping would have to be for the pizza to be perfectly symmetric: reflected across the best mirror axis, or turned by the best rotation. Green: a piece of the same topping lies there (within half the two pieces' combined radius; each piece partners one other), so the piece counts as matched. The share of green rings is the match; chance is the share the same pieces get at random positions with the same search; score = (match − chance) / (1 − chance), so 0 is no more symmetric than chance and 1 is every piece matched. Full definitions are on the Methods page. The contact panel joins pieces whose outlines touch or overlap; faded pieces touch nothing, and a number gives the height of a pile of three or more. A negative gap means pieces overlap. Groups come from the Neurotype (self-described) column.
