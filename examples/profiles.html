Pizza profiles and clusters

Each participant's pizza is summarised as a profile of 30 measures in 9 areas. Each score is standardised across these 60 participants (0 = sample average, ±1 = one standard deviation) and signed so that higher always means more of the named trait.

Clusters

No clear clusters: 60 pizzas, best mean silhouette 0.19 (k = 2). Hierarchical clustering always splits people into groups, wanted or not: the split is reported only with at least 20 pizzas and a mean silhouette of at least 0.25 (Kaufman & Rousseeuw 1990: below that there is no substantial structure).
