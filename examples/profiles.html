Pizza profiles and clusters

Each participant's pizza is summarised as a profile of 33 measures in 10 areas. Each score is standardised across these 60 participants (0 = sample average, ±1 = one standard deviation) and signed so that higher always means more of the named trait. Profiles are compared by their Euclidean distance and grouped with hierarchical clustering (Ward's method). The number of clusters, 2, is the one with the best average silhouette (k=2: 0.19 · k=3: 0.15 · k=4: 0.08 · k=5: 0.07 · k=6: 0.07 · k=7: 0.08 · k=8: 0.08); higher is better, and values under about 0.25 mean the clusters overlap.
